Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

The short version: Paperlight does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data. Your documents and signatures stay on your device.

This policy explains how the Paperlight app ("Paperlight", "the app"), developed by Jaloliddin Boltaev ("we", "us"), handles information.

Information we collect

None. Paperlight has no user accounts, no servers that receive your data, and no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Your documents and signatures

Scans, imported photos and PDFs, your edits, and saved signatures are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, this data is included in those backups under Apple's terms.

Camera and photos

Paperlight uses the camera only to scan documents when you choose to. Photos you import are selected through the system photo picker, so Paperlight can only see the items you pick.

Text recognition

Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks.

Purchases

Paperlight Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, email, payment details or other personal information. The app only receives confirmation from Apple of whether Pro is active.

Sharing

Files leave Paperlight only when you export or share them yourself, for example by Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files. The app or service you choose handles them under its own privacy policy.

Children

Paperlight does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all Paperlight data at any time by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
