Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a document?

Tap Scan and hold your iPhone over the page. Paperlight finds the edges and captures automatically; keep going for more pages, then tap Save. You can also import photos or PDFs with the + button.

My scan has shadows or looks gray.

Open the document, tap Filters, and choose White Paper. Use Crop to adjust the page edges if needed. Edits can always be changed later.

How do I add my signature?

Open a document and tap Sign. Draw a signature, or (with Pro) cut out your real signature from a photo or a scanned page. Tap the page to place it, drag to move, and use the corner handle to resize.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

Tap + → Get Paperlight Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → Paperlight Pro → Cancel Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the current period.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my documents stored? Are they backed up?

Documents are stored only on your iPhone, inside Paperlight. They are included in your regular iCloud or computer backups of the device. To keep a copy elsewhere, use Export and save to Files.
